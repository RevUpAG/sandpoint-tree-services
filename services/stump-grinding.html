Stump Grinding & Cleanup

A leftover stump is a tripping hazard, an eyesore and an obstacle for mowing, planting or building. Sand Point Tree Services grinds stumps in Titusville and across Brevard County so you can finally use that part of your yard again.

We use specialized equipment to grind the stump below ground level, take care to protect the plants and lawn around it, and remove the debris and wood chips so your property is left clean.

When to call us

  • Stumps left behind after an old tree removal
  • Stumps in the way of mowing or new landscaping
  • Areas you want to replant or build on
  • Stumps that are sprouting new shoots

FAQ

Stump Grinding FAQ

How does stump grinding work?

Stump grinding involves using specialized equipment to grind the remaining stump below ground level, eliminating tripping hazards and preparing the area for replanting or construction.

Is site cleanup included in stump grinding?

Yes, our service includes thorough removal of debris and wood chips resulting from stump grinding to leave your property clean.

How long does a typical stump grinding job take?

The duration depends on the size and number of stumps. Smaller jobs can be completed within a few hours, while larger projects may take longer.

Is there any damage to surrounding plants during stump removal?

Our team takes care to minimize impact on surrounding landscape while efficiently removing stumps.

Do you serve areas like Cocoa and Port St. John?

Absolutely. We provide stump grinding and site cleanup services throughout Titusville and Brevard County, including Cocoa and Port St. John.
