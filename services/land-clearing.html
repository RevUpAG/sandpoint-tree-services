Land Clearing

Planning to build, add a driveway, open up a fence line or reclaim an overgrown part of your property? Sand Point Tree Services clears trees, brush and debris for residential and rural properties in Titusville and throughout Brevard County.

We clear the land thoroughly, keep safety first throughout the job, and finish with a full cleanup so the site is ready for construction, landscaping or whatever comes next.

When to call us

  • Lots that need to be cleared before construction
  • Overgrown fence lines and access paths

  • Areas cleared for a driveway, shed or pool

FAQ

Land Clearing FAQ

What does land clearing involve?

Land clearing includes removing trees, brush, and debris to prepare the land for construction or landscaping.

How long does land clearing typically take?

The duration depends on the size and complexity of the site but generally ranges from a few days to a week.

Is land clearing safe for the environment?

Yes, our team follows best practices to minimize environmental impact while ensuring safety and efficiency.

Can you assist with site cleanup after land clearing?

Absolutely. We provide thorough cleanup to leave your property ready for its next use.

Where do you offer land clearing?

We serve Titusville, Rockledge, Palm Bay, Port St. John, Mims, Cocoa and surrounding regions.
