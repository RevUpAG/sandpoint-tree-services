Tree Trimming in Titusville

Tree Trimming & Pruning

Regular trimming keeps your trees healthy, strong and good-looking, and it keeps heavy limbs away from your roof, driveway and power lines. Sand Point Tree Services trims and prunes oaks, shade trees and palms for homes and properties in Titusville and throughout Brevard County.

We remove dead and weak branches, thin crowded canopies, raise low limbs and shape trees so they fit your landscape. Every job ends the same way: the branches and debris are cleaned up and hauled off.

When to call us

  • Branches hanging over your roof, pool or driveway
  • Dead, broken or weak limbs
  • A dense canopy that blocks light and wind
  • Palms with dead fronds or seed pods
  • Low limbs blocking walkways, the street or your view

FAQ

Tree Trimming FAQ

How often should I have my trees pruned?

The frequency depends on the tree species and growth rate. Generally, annual or bi-annual pruning helps maintain optimal health.

Will pruning harm my trees?

When done correctly by experienced arborists, pruning promotes healthy growth and does not harm your trees.

Can you help with storm-damaged trees?

Yes, we respond promptly to storm damage to assess and safely prune or remove affected trees.

Do you serve areas outside Titusville?

Absolutely. We provide tree pruning and canopy care services throughout Rockledge, Cocoa, Port St. John, Palm Bay, Mims and surrounding areas.
